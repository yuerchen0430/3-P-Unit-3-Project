Chucks Sandwitches

Ingredients Measurement
Bread 2 Slices
Ham As Many as You See Fit
Cheese (any kind) Number of Ham Slices Halved

Steps

1) Toast the bread on each side, set asside

2) Mayo on both slides of bread, one side each

3) Put cheese and ham slices interchanging on a bread slice, mayo touching ham/cheese.

4) Put other toast slice on top and grill again to melt cheese

Steak & Potatos

Ingredients Measurement
Beef 1 1/2 lbs
Potatos 1 lb
Butter 4 tbsp
Seasoning Meausure With Your Heart

Steps

1) olive oil and 2 tbsp of the butter in a pan. Let the butter melt.

2) Add the cut potatoes, garlic, rosemary, oregano, salt, and pepper. Cook for 4 minutes. Remove the potatoes from the pan and set aside in a bowl.

3) Return the skillet to the stove and increase the heat to high. Add 2 tbsp of butter and stir to melt.

4) Add the steak bites in and cook for 1 minute. Stir the steak for another 1-2 minutes.

5) Add the cooked potatoes back to the skillet and toss together. More seasoning.

6) Top with fresh chopped parsley
